# Fracturing Points

*Fracturing Points* is a psychological fiction concept centered on what it feels like when extreme emotional distress, life-threatening events, or traumatic experiences seem to break a person's connection to reality.

The story follows the idea that, after a severe fracture, someone may not stay in the same world they knew before. Instead, they move into an almost identical reality where the differences are subtle at first: objects are missing, people remember conversations differently, and familiar places no longer feel completely true. With each new fracture, those distortions grow stronger, making it harder to understand who you are, what is real, and how the world is supposed to work.

Inspired by an experience that affected the author's father, the book explores identity, memory, trauma, and the terrifying possibility that reality can shift without warning.
